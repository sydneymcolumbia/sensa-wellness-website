# Off-Site GEO: Concrete Outreach Target List

Created 2026-08-16. This executes the target strategy in geo-offsite-playbook.md.
Use the brand fact sheet and outreach copy from that playbook verbatim; this file
is the who-and-where. Update the Status column as outreach happens.

The pitch angle that runs through everything: every at-home CRP test covered by
health media today is a finger-prick blood sample mailed to a lab with results in
days. Sensa is saliva-based, needle-free, and gives a score in about 15 minutes
at home. That is a genuinely new category entry, not a me-too product. Lead with it.

**Entity warning (important):** there is no "Sensa Wellness" on Crunchbase,
Wikidata, or Product Hunt today, and the name space is crowded: "Sensa Health"
(a CBT mental-health app) has a Crunchbase profile, "Sensa (diet)" has a
Wikipedia article, and sensa.io is a fermentation company. Until our own
entries exist, AI knowledge graphs can confuse or dilute the brand. Tier 2 is
therefore not optional housekeeping; it is disambiguation defense. Always use
the full name "Sensa Wellness" plus the phrase "at-home CRP test" in every
profile and answer.
