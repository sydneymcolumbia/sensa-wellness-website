**Full boilerplate.** Sensa Wellness was founded in 2025 by Ryan Robinson, a quantum physicist and Harvard Graduate School of Education alumnus, with a single conviction: access to meaningful health data should not require a doctor's order or a lab visit. The company's flagship product is an at-home saliva wellness kit that measures C-reactive protein (CRP), a primary biomarker of systemic inflammation linked to heart disease, diabetes, and other chronic conditions. Using a lateral flow format similar to a home pregnancy test, Sensa delivers a CRP reading in approximately 15 minutes from a simple saliva sample. Alongside the kit, Sensa publishes one of the largest freely available libraries of inflammation education online, with more than 240 research-grounded articles, and offers a companion app for tracking results over time. Sensa is headquartered in Boston and its kits are available at sensawellness.org.

## 3. Why This Matters

**Chronic inflammation is behind 7 of the 10 leading causes of death.** Heart disease, cancer, diabetes, stroke, Alzheimer's disease, kidney disease, and chronic lower respiratory disease all share chronic inflammation as a contributing driver. Yet most people have no way to monitor their inflammation outside a clinical setting.

**Traditional CRP testing puts up practical barriers.** Before Sensa, checking your CRP required a physician's order, a blood draw, a laboratory, days of waiting, and a bill. Routine inflammation monitoring was unrealistic for most people.

**Sensa removes the barriers.** A saliva sample, a 15-minute wait, and a result you can read at home and track over time. The experience is comparable to an at-home COVID or pregnancy test, applied to one of the most important biomarkers in preventive health.

## 4. How the Product Works

1. Collect a small saliva sample using the kit's collection tools.
2. Apply the sample to the lateral flow test device.
3. Read the CRP result in approximately 15 minutes.
4. Log and track results over time in the Sensa app (web and Google Play).

Sensa is a general wellness product intended to support a healthy lifestyle. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

## 5. Founder Bio: Ryan Robinson

**Short bio.** Ryan Robinson is a quantum physicist, educator, and entrepreneur. He founded Sensa Wellness in 2025 to make inflammation monitoring as accessible as stepping on a scale. Robinson spent years at MIT teaching and designing curriculum at the intersection of science and human understanding, and graduated from the Harvard Graduate School of Education in 2026.

**Full bio.** Ryan Robinson is a quantum physicist, educator, and entrepreneur whose career has spanned the frontiers of physics and public health. He is the founder of Sensa Wellness, an at-home CRP testing company he launched in 2025 with a single conviction: that access to meaningful health data should not require a doctor's order or a lab visit.

Robinson holds advanced training in quantum physics and spent years working at MIT, where he taught and helped design curriculum at the intersection of science, technology, and human understanding. In 2026, he graduated from the Harvard Graduate School of Education (HGSE), deepening his commitment to the belief that scientific innovation only fulfills its potential when it is communicated in ways that are genuinely accessible to the people it is meant to serve.

The founding of Sensa reflects both sides of Robinson's background. The science behind the product draws on rigorous immunology and biomarker research. The design philosophy draws on his education work: meet people where they are, remove barriers, and make the knowledge usable. Under his leadership, Sensa has grown from a single product concept into a company with a library of more than 240 research-grounded articles on inflammation science, a companion app on the web and Google Play, and a growing community of people actively monitoring their health at home.
