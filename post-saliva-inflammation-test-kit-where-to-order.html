Saliva Inflammation Test Kit: Where to Order One

Nearly every at-home inflammation test for sale today asks for a drop of blood from a finger prick. Saliva is the exception, and as far as we can tell there is exactly one consumer kit that uses it. Here is where to order it, what it costs, and the cases where a blood test is the smarter purchase.

Written by Sydney Murphy, CMO & Digital Products Officer. Reviewed by the Sensa Wellness editorial team.Sensa pricing and ordering details verified against this site on November 4, 2026; competitor figures last verified on the sellers' own websites on November 3, 2026.

The short answer

Sensa is the only consumer saliva inflammation test kit we are aware of, and it is sold direct from this site rather than through Amazon, a pharmacy or any other retailer. You swab saliva into a single-use vial, the reagent inside changes color, and the Sensa app reads the vial with your phone camera to produce a 0 to 100 wellness score in 15 to 30 minutes, with no needle and no lab. It is a general wellness product for tracking your own trend over time, not a diagnostic test. If you need a laboratory hs-CRP value for a doctor, order a blood test instead, and we name the cheapest ones below.

A note before anything else: this article lives on the Sensa Wellness site, and Sensa is our product. We have tried to write the version that is actually useful rather than the version that sells hardest, which means being plain about what a saliva kit does not do and naming the situations where somebody else's blood test is the better buy.

The reason this search is frustrating is that the category barely exists. Saliva collection is routine in laboratory research and common in mail-in hormone panels, but the consumer inflammation market runs on blood. The familiar names, Everlywell and myLAB Box, both ship a lancet and a collection card. Laboratory suppliers do sell salivary CRP assays, but those are sold to research labs for research use, not to people at home. That leaves one place to order a saliva inflammation kit, which is a strange thing for us to be able to say and worth stating plainly rather than dressing up.

What a Saliva Inflammation Test Kit Actually Is

The whole kit is a vial, a reagent and your phone.You open the single-use vial, swab saliva into it, and give the reagent a few minutes to react to the CRP present in the sample. The color shifts, and you scan the vial with the Sensa app, which reads the color with your phone camera and converts it into a number. There is no lancet, no finger prick, no blood draw, no collection card to dry, nothing to mail, no countertop device to buy, no batteries and no power outlet. Kits are shelf-stable at room temperature, so a multi-pack sits in a cupboard until the day you want to use one.

What comes back is a wellness score, not a laboratory value.The app gives you a score from 0 to 100 in one of three tiers: In range, Worth watching, or Time to reset. That score is not an hs-CRP value and it should never be read against blood reference ranges or clinical cutoffs, because saliva CRP and blood hs-CRP are different fluids measured on entirely different scales. Our research explainer on measuring CRP in salivagoes into why, and hs-CRP versus standard CRPcovers the blood assays. The value of a score is in repetition: one reading tells you very little, while a line of readings across the weeks you changed your sleep, your drinking or your training tells you which direction you are heading.

If You Need a Laboratory Number, Order Blood Instead

A saliva score will not satisfy a doctor who asked for an hs-CRP.This is the single most important thing on the page. If a clinician requested the test, if you are following up on a previously elevated result, or if you want a number to take to an appointment, you need a laboratory value in milligrams per liter, and a blood test is the right purchase. The cheapest routes to one, last verified on the sellers' own websites on November 3, 2026, are Quest Health's hs-CRP test at $65 plus a $6 physician fee and Labcorp OnDemand's hs-CRP test at $69, both completed with a venous draw at a collection site. What a Saliva Kit Will Not Tell You

Sensa is a general wellness product, and the limits matter more than the features.It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease or condition, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified provider. The score is not an hs-CRP value, so it cannot be compared with blood reference ranges, plotted against clinical cutoffs or used in place of a test your doctor ordered. It will not tell you why a reading moved, only that it did, and a single high reading has plenty of mundane explanations, from a cold to a hard workout. If a reading worries you, or if you have symptoms, the next step is a clinician rather than another kit.
