Sydney Murphy

CMO & Digital Products Officer, Sensa Wellness

Sydney leads marketing and digital product at Sensa Wellness. She built the Sensa website, web app experience, and content program, and she writes and edits the articles in the Sensa inflammation library with the Sensa editorial team.

Her interest in inflammation is personal. Growing up with a lung condition that kept her airways chronically inflamed, she was offered a lifetime of medication or the harder path of changing how she ate and trained. She chose the second, watched her inflammation fall over the years, and now carries an inhaler only for peace of mind. That experience, seeing what becomes possible when you can finally track what is happening inside your body, is why she works on making inflammation visible to everyone.

Her writing focuses on C-reactive protein and what it means for everyday wellness: how lifestyle, food, sleep, stress, and exercise move the number, and how to read trends rather than panic over single results. Everything published under her byline follows the Sensa editorial standards and is reviewed before it goes live.
