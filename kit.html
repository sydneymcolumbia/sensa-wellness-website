How Sensa works

A few minutes of hands-on time, then a 15 to 30 minute wait. Your score appears in the app as soon as you scan.

01

Collect

Swab the inside of your mouth to gather saliva. No blood, no needles.

02

Activate

Pour the activator into the clear vial.

03

Mix and wait

Place the swab in the vial, squeeze it against the inside wall so your saliva mixes in, cap it, and wait 15 to 30 minutes.

04

Scan

Tap Scan in the Sensa app and hold the vial in frame. Your reading is logged to your history.

The whole process, start to finish. Filmed with the kit that ships today.
