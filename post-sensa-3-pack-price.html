What the three-pack costs, what it works out to per test, how it compares with the single kit and the four-pack, and the situation where a different purchase is the better buy.

The short answer

The Sensa 3-Pack Progress Tracker is $79 for three at-home test kits, which works out to about $26 per test and saves $38 against buying three single kits at $39 each. Shipping is free inside the United States, there is no subscription, and there is nothing added at checkout. Sensa is a general wellness kit for tracking your own trend over time and is not a diagnostic test, so if you need a laboratory hs-CRP number for a doctor, buy a lab test instead.

Why Three Tests Is the Smallest Pack That Tells You Anything

One reading is a number with nothing to compare it against. A single Sensa score tells you where your saliva CRP reading landed on one morning, and saliva CRP moves with sleep, illness, training load and plenty else. That is an odd thing for a seller to write, but it is the honest basis for recommending three tests rather than one: the value of the product is the direction the readings move in, not any one of them.

Three readings is the minimum sequence that shows direction. The pattern the three-pack is built around is simple. Take a baseline reading before you change anything. Change one thing, whether that is a sleep schedule, alcohol, training volume or how you eat, and give it a few weeks. Take a second reading. Then take a third, because two points can be coincidence and the third tells you whether the move held. That is the whole pack, and it is why the bundle is named the Progress Tracker rather than the Value Pack.

Spacing matters as much as the number of tests. Testing three times in one week mostly measures the noise rather than the trend, and spacing readings months apart means too much has changed in between to attribute anything. Our guide to how often to test inflammation works through that trade-off, and whether CRP can be measured in saliva covers what the research behind the method does and does not support.

What Comes in the Three-Pack

Three single-use kits, the app that reads them, and shipping. Each kit holds everything one test needs, and the Sensa app turns the color change in the vial into a 0 to 100 wellness score in one of three tiers: In range, Worth watching, or Time to reset. Every reading is time-stamped and saved to your history in the app, which is what makes three readings more useful than three separate numbers on paper. Our kit page walks through the four steps with a short demo video.

There is nothing else to buy and nothing to maintain. The kits are shelf-stable at room temperature, so there is no refrigeration to arrange and no reason to order all three at the moment you plan to use them. There are no batteries, no reader and no dock, because the app uses your phone camera. The only thing you need that is not in the box is a smartphone.

There is no subscription attached to any pack. Buying the three-pack starts no plan and renews nothing. The app keeps your result history whether or not you buy again, and if you use two kits and leave the third on a shelf for six months, nothing expires on you in the meantime.

Three-Pack, Single Kit or Four-Pack

The three-pack is the middle option, and the choice comes down to how many readings you will actually take. The per-test cost is effectively the same for the three-pack and the four-pack, about $26 either way, so this is not really a price decision between those two. It is a question of whether you want three readings or four.

When a Different Purchase Beats the Three-Pack

If you need a laboratory number, $79 spent on a three-pack buys something that cannot answer your question. A Sensa result is a 0 to 100 wellness score in one of three tiers. It is not an hs-CRP value, it is not reported in milligrams per liter, and it should never be read against blood reference ranges. So if your doctor asked for an hs-CRP, or you want a result to take to an appointment, buy a blood test. A single draw-site test is also cheaper than the three-pack: Quest Health listed hs-CRP at $65 plus a $6 physician fee and Labcorp OnDemand at $69 when we last verified them on November 3, 2026, both below $79.
