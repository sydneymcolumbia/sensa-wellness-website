Does Sensa Wellness Ship for Free?

The shipping policy in plain terms: what it costs, how long it takes, where we ship, what shows up at your door, and how to store the kit until you use it.

Written by Sydney Murphy, CMO & Digital Products Officer. Reviewed by the Sensa Wellness editorial team. Sensa shipping terms and prices verified against sensawellness.org on November 11, 2026. Competitor details were last verified on the sellers' own websites on November 3, 2026 and could not be re-checked on November 11, 2026, so every competitor figure below carries that earlier date.

The short answer

Yes. Shipping is free on every Sensa order inside the United States, with no minimum, no handling fee and no code to enter. At the time of writing, on November 11, 2026, the order page lists $39 for a single kit, $79 for the three-pack and $103 for the four-pack, and those are the full amounts you pay. Kits are assembled to order and ship within two weeks, with tracking emailed the day yours goes out, and nothing ever has to be mailed back because there is no lab in the loop. Sensa is a general wellness kit: you spit into a single-use vial, the reagent changes color, and the Sensa app reads the vial with your phone camera to give you a 0 to 100 wellness score in about 15 minutes. It tracks your own trend over time and is not a diagnostic test, so if you need a laboratory hs-CRP number for a doctor, buy a lab test instead.

Yes, Shipping Is Free on Every Order

Free shipping applies to all three bundles, with no minimum order. You do not have to buy a multi-pack to qualify, there is no handling or packaging charge, and there is no shipping line added at the end of checkout. Free shipping is not a promotion you have to find: it is the standing policy, applied automatically, with no code to type in. The price on the bundle is the price that hits your card.

How Long Delivery Takes

Kits ship within two weeks of your order. That window exists because kits are assembled to order rather than pulled off a warehouse shelf. The day yours goes out we email you tracking, so you are not left guessing. For the holidays, the site states that orders placed by December 5 arrive in time for Christmas.

You pay today, and you can cancel until it ships. Checkout runs through Stripe and charges your card at the time of the order. If you change your mind at any point before your kit leaves us, email info@sensawellness.org and we refund the order in full. If a batch sells out, the order page switches to pre-order wording: you are still charged at checkout, your kit ships with the next batch, and we email you the shipping date, with the same full refund available any time before shipment.

Where Sensa Ships

Inside the United States only, at the moment. Checkout collects United States shipping addresses, so we cannot take an international order today, and we would rather say that plainly than let you reach the payment screen and find out. We do not currently ship to addresses outside the US, including Canada.

If you are outside the US, a mail-in lab kit is the more likely route. Several of the sellers covered in our roundup of at-home CRP tests operate in other countries, and their own checkout pages are the place to confirm whether they deliver to yours. We did not re-verify their shipping destinations for this article, so treat that as a pointer rather than a promise.

What Arrives, and What You Never Have to Mail Back

One box, one direction. Each Sensa kit is a single-use saliva test: a vial, the activator, a swab and the instructions. You collect saliva with the swab, add it to the activator in the vial, cap it, wait while the color develops, and scan the vial in the Sensa app, which runs in the browser on any phone with a camera. There is no needle, no finger prick, no blood, no lab requisition and no sample to send anywhere.

That is the quiet difference in the shipping story. A mail-in kit has to make a round trip: the kit travels to you, then your sample travels to a laboratory before anything can be measured. According to Everlywell's website, verified on October 27, 2026, its Vitamin D & Inflammation Test includes prepaid shipping in both directions, and myLAB Box's At Home Inflammation Test includes a prepaid return envelope, verified on November 3, 2026. Prepaid is not the same as instant, though. The return leg is the main reason a mail-in result takes days rather than minutes. Our page on Sensa versus mail-in blood test kits compares the two approaches in full, and Sensa versus Everlywell works through one of them in detail.

Storing the Kit Once It Arrives

Put it in a cupboard and forget about it. Each single-use Sensa device is shelf-stable at room temperature. There is no refrigeration, no batteries and no equipment to maintain, which is deliberate: it keeps the cost down and it means a multi-pack can sit on a shelf until you want your next reading. Keep the vial capped and sealed until you are ready to test, and keep it out of direct sunlight and away from heat, as you would any other reagent.

This is why a multi-pack makes sense for trend tracking. One reading is a single data point. The value of Sensa is the line those readings draw across the weeks you changed your sleep, your training or your diet, and that only works if your next test is already in the house. When Free Shipping Is Not the Thing That Should Decide It

If you need a laboratory hs-CRP number, buy the lab test, whatever the postage costs. Chronic low-grade inflammation is assessed clinically with high-sensitivity CRP in blood, reported in milligrams per liter against the reference ranges your doctor uses. A Sensa score is not that number. It is a 0 to 100 wellness score from saliva, placed in one of three tiers, and it should never be read against blood reference ranges. If a clinician asked you for an hs-CRP, the honest recommendation is the draw-site route, where nothing ships at all and the price is lower than either mail-in kit: Labcorp OnDemand at $69 or Quest Health at $65 plus a $6 physician fee, both as verified on November 3, 2026. Our guide to at-home hs-CRP kits with lab results covers those options, and hs-CRP versus standard CRP explains why the distinction matters.

Where free shipping genuinely helps is frequency. If what you want is to see whether a change in sleep, food, training or stress shows up in your body, you need readings you can take often and cheaply, and postage on every order would quietly undo that. Free shipping on a $26 per test multi-pack is what makes a weekly or fortnightly habit practical.
